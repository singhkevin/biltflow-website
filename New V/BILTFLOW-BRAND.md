# Biltflow — Brand Guide (v1.1, September 2026)

Paste this whole file into Claude (chat or Code) as the style contract for anything Biltflow: websites, product UI, decks, documents, ads.

## 1. What Biltflow is
- Biltflow is the **AI-native operating system for high-density residential development + construction**. Entity: Hunt Global Pte Ltd (formerly ArchieTech OS™). Markets: NSW, QLD, VIC. Australian English.
- Position: the **connective operating layer** across the whole lifecycle — connects the tools customers already run, standardises workflows, adds a construction-trained AI workforce. Integration-first, never "rip and replace". Portfolio-level language, not project-level.
- Positioning statement (use verbatim): *Biltflow is the construction and development operating system that connects the tools you already run, standardises the way work happens, and puts a construction-trained AI workforce across the whole lifecycle — so every project makes the next one smarter.*
- Feeling to engineer: **NOISE → SILENCE → SIGNAL.** Move the audience from fragmentation, through a deliberate pause, into precision. Space and restraint are the brand.
- We are: an operating system, AI-native from the ground up, trained on $5B+ of real projects, integration-first, portfolio-level.
- We are not: a dashboard to look at, a generic AI bolted onto old software, a humanoid chatbot, futuristic for its own sake, loud or playful.

## 2. Voice
- Short declarative sentences. Full stops. Deliberate repetition. A construction director briefing a room, not a SaaS landing page.
- Hero idea 01: **"The tools changed. The way construction operates didn't. Until now."**
- Hero idea 02: **"The next project already knows."** (always give it air — no button beside it, nothing stacked on it)
- Line library: "One system. All projects. All data. All connected." · "Start anywhere. Connect everything." · "Not disconnected AI tools. One coordinated digital workforce." · "Connecting what happened to why it happened." · "Build. Learn. Improve. Repeat."
- Use: operating system, lifecycle, intelligence layer, digital workforce, orchestration, AI-native, module, connector, programme, portfolio, standardise, in control.
- Avoid: revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, solution, empower, cutting-edge, next-generation, "AI-powered" (say AI-native), "rip and replace", "unlock your potential". No exclamation marks. No emoji. No italics.
- Naming: **Biltflow** in running copy (never BiltFlow/BILTFLOW except as artwork). **EVE** all caps. Agents Title Case (Tango, Cash, Sophia, Fred, Wilma). "Programme" = construction schedule. Sentence case for UI and headings.

## 5. Typography
- **Archivo** (variable: wght 100–900, wdth 62–125) for everything except data. Google Fonts: `family=Archivo:wdth,wght@62..125,100..900`.
- **JetBrains Mono** (300–700) for eyebrows, labels, IDs, dates, currency, percentages, timecodes. `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` in tables.
- Fallbacks: `Archivo, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` · `"JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace`.
- Scale (web): display-xl 56–84px / 1.02–1.06 / wght 200 / wdth 118 / ls −0.015em (one per page) · display-l 44 / 1.12 / 300 / 112 · display-m 34 / 1.15 / 400 / 112 · heading-s 24 / 1.3 / 500 · lede 17 / 1.6 · body 15 / 1.65 (max 68ch) · eyebrow mono 11 / ls 0.22em / uppercase / accent · label mono 10 / ls 0.16em / uppercase / muted · metric 38–104 / 1 / wght 200–300 / tabular.
- Rules: uppercase always tracked ≥0.14em; never above weight 600; no italics; no third face, no serif, no condensed; no gradient/outlined text; slides ≥24px; print ≥10pt; `text-wrap: pretty` on body, `balance` on headlines.

## 6. Layout
- Container 1240px, 40px page margins, 12 columns, 24px gutter (8 cols tablet, 4 mobile). Breakpoints 480 / 768 / 1024 / 1440.
- Spacing (4px base): 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96 128. Card padding 24–30; section rhythm 96–112; hero breathing room 128.
- Radius: 2 inputs · 4 buttons/chips · 6 cards/panels · 8–10 media/modals · pill for filter chips and avatars. Nothing else above 10px.
- Depth = 1px hairlines + surface ladder. **No shadows** except one overlay shadow `0 16px 48px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`. No card lift on hover — hover = border colour shift to the accent.
- Touch targets ≥44px. Buttons 40–44px tall.

## 7. Components (spec)
- Primary button: ultra-500 fill, white text, 1px same-colour border, radius 4, 14–15px/500, hover ultra-600 (light) or ultra-400 (dark). One primary per view. Labels are verbs, sentence case.
- Secondary: transparent/white fill, 1px control border, hover border→accent. Tertiary: accent text + "→".
- Inputs: radius 2, control border, focus border = accent, 11–13px padding. Error border danger.
- Status chip: 3px radius, dot + mono uppercase 10px, tinted bg/border per table above. Filter chip: pill.
- Table: mono uppercase header on ink-800 (dark) / chalk-100 (light); rows 48px (40 compact); selected row = accent tint (ultra-800 dark / ultra-100 light), never a border; totals under a heavier control-border rule.
- KPI: mono label + Archivo metric (300, wdth 112, tabular) + mono delta.
- **Mode control** (brand signature): segmented MANUAL / COPILOT / AUTOPILOT, mono uppercase, active segment ultra-500 (light) or ultra-300 with ink text (dark). Default Copilot. Appears wherever autonomy is discussed.
- **EVE prompt bar**: 1px accent border, 5–7-bar waveform icon, "EVE" label. EVE is a waveform + label — never a face, orb, avatar or robot.
- Integration tiles: partner names in equal neutral tiles — never greyed, broken or ranked below Biltflow.

## 8. Brand systems (fixed furniture)
- **Lifecycle line** — 13 nodes on ONE continuous line: Site sourcing → Feasibility → Approvals → Design → Estimating → Procurement → Commercial → Construction → Compliance → Handover → Defects → Maintenance → Learning. Hollow nodes; exactly one filled accent node marks focus. Never boxes, cards or a funnel. Where possible the line returns to the start (Learning closes the loop).
- **EVE** — orchestration layer; first person; offers control before action ("You stay in control. Or, within your approved workflows, I can take action.").
- **Agents** — 20+ specialists, one coordinated workforce. Named agents own a relationship and speak (Tango · opportunity, Cash · execution, Sophia · defects & DLP, Fred · sourcing, Wilma · suppliers). Functional agents are utilities and never speak (Cost Agent, QA Agent, Safety Agent, Programme, Design). No faces, characters or hero-team line-ups.
- **Traceability chain** — backward arrows: Defect ← Installation ← Subcontractor ← Product ← Supplier ← Procurement ← Design; reverse it forward into "Project 02" to show compounding intelligence. Use sparingly.
- **$5B+** — always its own band: giant thin numeral + mono caption "Real high-density residential construction project data". No card, no icon, no neighbouring stat.

## 9. Motion
- Principle: Noise → Silence → Signal. Movement equals meaning.
- Durations: 120ms hover/focus · 200ms panel/tab/row · 320ms route/modal · 600ms data along the lifecycle line · ≤1200ms hero reveal (once). Easing `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; no spring, bounce or overshoot.
- Permitted on websites: scroll-reveal fade + ≤28px rise with ≤90ms stagger; the lifecycle line drawing itself into view; gentle parallax on large photographs (≤5% travel); border/tint hover shifts; the EVE waveform (the only self-running animation) and a single slow pulse on agent activity.
- Never: animate the logo (it appears, it does not assemble/explode/particle), parallax layers on type, scroll-jacking, counters ticking on every stat, looping gradients or "AI shimmer", card lift-and-shadow, more than one element moving at a time. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (opacity only).

## 10. Imagery
- Two things only: **genuine construction environments** and **genuine Biltflow screens**. Australian high-density sites (towers, façades, cores, formwork, fit-out); real practitioners at work, never smiling at camera; PPE because the site needs it, not as props; finished-building imagery warmer (lobbies, apartments, residents).
- Grade: before-Biltflow = warmer, grainier, crowded, handheld; after-Biltflow = cool, clean, high-contrast, locked-off, one subject per frame.
- Generation (Higgsfield): `soul_location` for sites/interiors/architecture, `soul_2` for people, `nano_banana_pro` for UI/dashboard plates. Always end prompts with "no text, no logos"; specify Australian context and the grade above. Treat generated UI plates as look-and-feel only — real UI is built in HTML.
- Banned: brains/neural nets/circuits/robots, any face or orb standing in for EVE, isometric 3D city illustrations, "diverse team pointing at a laptop" stock, impossible UI, AI likenesses of real people, competitors shown broken, purple/cyan gradients.
- Scrim for type over photos: `rgba(0,0,4,.7)` dark / `rgba(255,255,255,.82)` light.

## 11. Website patterns (biltflow.com)
- Theme split ≈ **75% Chalk / 25% dark**. Dark is reserved for the EVE + $5B+ section and the closing CTA/footer.
- Hero is **dashboard-led**: compact one-line headline + eyebrow chip left, two CTAs right, then a full-container-width 16:9 platform video frame (poster fallback, play control hides only once playback starts). Light ultramarine wash (#E9EAFF→#F7F9FA) behind.
- Section order: Hero → "Works with" strip → Problem (photo + 3 iconed points) → Lifecycle (line + Start anywhere / Connect everything / Replace when ready) → full-bleed statement band → Who it's for (6 persona doors: Executives, Construction, Commercial, Development, Site teams, Digital/IT — 3 with portraits) → EVE (dark; product panel + mode control + agent chips + $5B+) → Asset classes (High-density residential, Build-to-rent, Hotels & serviced apartments, Mixed-use; 2×2 photo cards) → "The next project already knows" (traceability chain + drawings photo) → Human close (full-bleed photo) → Closing CTA (dark) → footer.
- Icons: thin line, 1.5px stroke, round caps, 22–24px, accent-coloured. No filled or duotone icon sets.
- Closing CTA: logo, mono descriptor, "Build. Learn. Improve. Repeat.", one primary + one secondary button, then **biltflow.com** as the CTA. No app-store badges, no QR code. "Powered by EVE" very small.

## 13. Converting a stock/Envato template (in this order)
1. Swap fonts: Archivo (set the width axis on display type) + JetBrains Mono for anything numeric. 2. Set the surface ladder (Chalk or dark) and delete white-on-grey card mixes. 3. Delete every shadow → 1px borders. 4. Clamp radii to 4/6/10/pill. 5. One accent: map primary → ultra-500, secondary → neutral. 6. Thin line icons only. 7. Replace illustrations/gradients with real photography or a real screen. 8. Rewrite headings as short declaratives with full stops. 9. One primary CTA per section. 10. Add the lifecycle line and the Manual/Copilot/Autopilot control.

## 14. Eight decisions that keep it on brand
Do: start from the surface ladder and add only what earns its place · let one ultramarine element carry the meaning · headlines light/wide/large, data in mono · real screens, drawings and numbers · give the hero lines and $5B+ their own space · hairlines for depth · name the pathway the visitor is on · end on the URL.
Don't: add a second accent or a purple/cyan gradient · round past 10px or shadow cards · bold/condensed/italic headlines · illustrate AI as a face/brain/orb/robot · fake a capability to fill a layout · stack three CTAs · animate the logo or the whole page at once · write "seamless", "revolutionary" or "AI-powered".
