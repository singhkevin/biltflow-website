1 What Market Is Biltflow Serving?
This pack deliberately treats the target market as broader than any single regulatory label.
Understanding why requires a short walk through the asset classes Biltflow is built for.
1.1 Class 2 and High-Density Residential
Under the National Construction Code, Class 2 buildings are buildings containing two or
more sole-occupancy units — typically apartment buildings. In New South Wales, regulated-
building obligations under the Design and Building Practitioners (DBP) Act now extend to
Class 2, Class 3 and Class 9c buildings, requiring registered design and building
practitioners to lodge regulated designs and compliance declarations on the NSW Planning
Portal. For Biltflow, the practical market is wider than this legal label: multi-storey
apartment projects, mixed-use towers, build-to-rent, hotels and serviced apartments all
share the same complex design, procurement, commercial, programme, quality and
handover workflows — which is why this pack maps all of them together.
1.2 What Is a Fully Integrated Builder-Developer?
A fully integrated builder-developer controls multiple stages of the property lifecycle inside
the same corporate group, rather than acting only as a developer that appoints an external
head contractor. The integrated group may acquire land, run feasibility, obtain planning
approvals, manage design, procure consultants and subcontractors, construct the project,
sell or lease the product, manage settlements and defects, and sometimes retain or operate
the completed asset.
— Why it matters to Biltflow: one customer can have development, construction,
commercial, finance, design, safety, procurement and operations teams all using
different systems and duplicating the same data.
— The opportunity is an integrated data and workflow layer connecting the project from
feasibility and design through procurement, delivery, commissioning, handover and
operations.
— Examples of integrated models include Deicorp in Sydney and Pace Development
Group in Melbourne, both of which describe end-to-end development and construction
capability.
1.3 High-Density Residential Construction
High-density residential construction generally means multi-storey apartment development
where many dwellings are delivered within one building or precinct. Projects typically
involve complex structures, façades, vertical transport, fire and life-safety systems, building
services, authority approvals, hundreds of subcontract packages, large drawing and model
sets, staged commissioning, and defects and purchaser or resident handover. This
complexity is precisely what makes these projects highly software-intensive — and why the
volume and complexity of the bill of quantities multiplies compared with detached housing.

Hotel projects combine complex construction with an operating business. In addition to
normal building delivery, teams coordinate brand standards, operator requirements, front-

1.5 Serviced Apartments
Serviced apartments sit between residential apartments and hotels. Individual units are
designed for short- or extended-stay accommodation and typically include kitchens or
kitchenettes, while the building may operate with hotel-style reception, housekeeping,
booking, maintenance and guest services. Meriton Suites is a well-known Australian
example of the integrated apartment / serviced-apartment model — and illustrates why a
single tower can require several different fit-out specifications and compliance classes to be
tracked within one construction programme.
1.6 Build-to-Rent (BTR)
Build-to-rent is purpose-built rental housing retained by an institutional owner or
investment vehicle rather than sold apartment-by-apartment. Because the owner normally
retains the asset, decisions made during design and construction directly affect long-term
leasing, maintenance, energy performance, resident experience and operating cost. That
creates a strong case for maintaining a continuous digital record from development and
construction through into operations — the exact thread that connects Biltflow's
development, delivery and operations use cases.

3 Who Buys Biltflow? Buyer and Influencer Role Map
Construction technology is rarely purchased by one person. Enterprise deals normally
require an executive sponsor, functional champions, IT/security approval, commercial
justification and end-user adoption. The most important website implication is that Biltflow
should speak to several audiences at once: executives want control and margin; project
teams want less admin; commercial teams want cost certainty; IT wants a secure, integrated
architecture; and site teams want speed and simplicity.

S E C T I O N 5
5 What the Biltflow Website Should Communicate
The market map suggests Biltflow should not present itself as another isolated project-
management application. The stronger position is a construction and development
operating system that can connect existing tools, standardise workflows, create a shared
data layer, and use AI across the full project lifecycle.
— Lead with the business problem: construction companies are running critical
workflows across disconnected systems, spreadsheets, inboxes, PDFs and project-
specific tools.
— Position Biltflow as the connective operating layer — not "rip and replace everything"
by default. Show integrations prominently.
— Show lifecycle coverage: Development → Design → Preconstruction → Estimating →
Procurement → Commercial → Construction → Safety & Quality → Commissioning →
Handover → Operations.
— Create persona pathways: For Executives, For Construction, For Commercial, For
Development, For Site Teams, For Digital/IT.
— Create asset-class pathways: High-Density Residential, Build-to-Rent, Hotels &
Serviced Apartments, Mixed-Use.
— Use portfolio-level language, not just project-level language: enterprise controls,
standardisation, cross-project intelligence, data governance, benchmarking and
forecasting.
— Make AI tangible: summarise project risk, interrogate contracts/drawings, predict
schedule/cost issues, automate workflows, surface decisions and create executive
intelligence from live project data.
— Use competitor websites primarily for information architecture and market education,
while making Biltflow visually more modern, integrated and AI-native.
