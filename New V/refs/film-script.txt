Daniel Craig

Jason Statham

unreasonable.

4. NEVER FAKE PRODUCT CAPABILITY
Every Biltflow interaction shown after 0:30 should either:
exist today, or
be clearly designed as a near-term product interaction that the engineering/product team has approved.
Do not create impossible futuristic UI simply because it looks good.
5. SHOW ACTION, NOT DASHBOARDS
The biggest danger after the Hollywood opening is falling into two minutes of dashboards.
Whenever possible show:
Biltflow finding something.
Biltflow connecting something.
Biltflow flagging something.
EVE making a decision.
Sophia tracing an issue.
A workflow completing.
A future project avoiding an old mistake.
The film is about an operating system that acts, not another place to look at information.
6. $5 BILLION SHOULD BE A HERO MOMENT
Do not bury this inside a paragraph.
Visually stop for:
$5B+
Then:
REAL CONSTRUCTION PROJECT DATA

THE TEST
Before approving the film, show it to someone who has never seen Biltflow.
Then ask them only three questions:
1. What problem does Biltflow solve?
They should answer:
"Construction uses too many disconnected processes, systems and sources of information."
2. What makes Biltflow different?
They should answer:
"It connects the whole construction lifecycle and uses construction-trained AI agents to understand and act across it."
3. What line do you remember?
Ideally:
"The tools changed. The way construction operates didn't."
or
"The next project already knows."
If they cannot answer those three questions after one viewing, simplify the film rather than adding more explanation.
