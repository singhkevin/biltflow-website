Construction-native

Intelligence built from real buildings, not theory.

EVE reads drawings, costs, contracts, programmes, site diaries and compliance records across every system you run — and understands how they relate. That understanding came from more than $5 billion of delivered high-density residential work.

Benchmarked against real cost plans, programmes and defects from completed towers. A clash in S-101 is connected to the RFI, the variation and the slab it will delay.
